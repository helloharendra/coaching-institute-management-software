## What Is Coaching Institute Management Software?

Coaching Institute Management Software is a digital platform designed to manage the academic and administrative activities of coaching centres and training institutes.

A customised solution can bring student records, admissions, batches, faculty, fees, attendance, examinations, study materials, and reporting together in one centralised system.

Naya Prayog Technologies develops tailored coaching management systems based on an institute's operational requirements.
